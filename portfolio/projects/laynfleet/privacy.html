LaynFleet Privacy Policy

Your Privacy & Data Practices

This Privacy Policy explains how Digilayn collects, uses, and protects personal information across LaynRider and LaynDriver. We believe in minimal data collection, role-based separation, and total transparency.

Effective date: September 1, 2026 • Compliant with South Africa POPIA and Mobile App Store Standards.

🛡️ In Plain English (Summary)

  • ✓ No Data Selling: We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information or trip logs to third parties or advertising brokers.
  • ✓ Rider Location Privacy: Riders are never tracked in the background. GPS is only queried once when you tap "Use Current Location" or enter a pickup address.
  • ✓ Driver Live Location: Drivers share real-time location while "Online" or executing trips so riders and dispatch radar can see vehicle availability.
  • ✓ Right to Erasure: You can request deletion in the app or use the web deletion page to inspect and immediately permanently delete your account data.

⚠️ A Community Platform — Used at Your Own Risk

  • • LaynFleet is built for the Poortjie community, where riders and drivers largely know one another. It is a tool to help neighbours arrange local rides.
  • • We do not verify the real identity of any user. Names, photos, and phone numbers are provided by users themselves and are not checked against official documents.
  • • Riders and drivers use the app at their own risk. You should only accept or offer rides to and from people you personally know and trust. Digilayn is a connection platform and is not responsible for the conduct of any user.

1. Information We Collect

🧑 LaynRider

Rider Information

  • Account Details: Your email address (used to sign in via email/password or Google Sign-In), your name, and a mobile phone number. Your phone number is stored so the driver matched to your trip can contact you — it is not verified by SMS or OTP.
  • Profile Photo: A profile picture you upload, stored securely and shown to the driver matched to your trip.
  • Trip & Booking Data: Requested pickup location, destination, fare quotation, ride status, and timestamps.
  • On-Demand Location: Geographic coordinates obtained strictly when you tap "Use Current Location" to set your pickup point. We do not monitor your location when you are not actively setting a trip.
  • Feedback & Ratings: Ratings (1–5 stars) and optional written feedback submitted after completed trips.
🚗 LaynDriver

Driver Information

  • Driver Verification: Full name, phone number, vehicle details (make, model, color, registration/plate number), and driver documentation.
  • Live Location Tracking (Foreground & Background): While your driver status is toggled to "Online" or during an active trip, LaynDriver collects continuous GPS location. This enables the dispatch radar to broadcast your vehicle to nearby riders and calculate accurate ETAs.
  • Performance & Governance: Trip completion records, rider ratings, acceptance rates, and participation in fleet pricing votes.
📱 All Users

Device & Technical Data

  • Device Registry: To keep your account secure and route notifications correctly, we record the devices you sign in on and let you pick a preferred device. Signing in on a new device can sign you out elsewhere.
  • Push Notification Tokens: A push token from Firebase Cloud Messaging (or Huawei Push) so we can send you trip and dispatch updates. We never send marketing spam.
  • Diagnostic Logs: Basic technical logs of app operations and errors, used only to investigate issues and fix crashes. We do not log passwords or secrets.

2. How Your Information Is Used

We process data solely to operate and improve the LaynFleet transport ecosystem:

📍 Trip Dispatch & Routing

Connecting riders with available drivers, rendering map routes, and displaying live vehicle arrival updates.

🛡️ Safety & Mutual Trust

Displaying driver and vehicle details to the rider, and showing the rider's name and photo to the matched driver, so both parties can recognise each other at pickup. We do not verify the real identity of any user.

🏷️ Fair Pricing & Governance

Calculating distance-based trip estimates and recording driver pricing votes.

🔧 Support & Debugging

Investigating booking issues, resolving ride disputes, and fixing application crashes.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

When a rider and driver are matched for a trip, they share the personal details needed to find and contact each other for the duration of that booking:

  • The driver sees the rider's name, profile photo, rating, phone number, and pickup location, and can call the rider from the app.
  • The rider sees the driver's name, profile photo, vehicle details and plate number, live location, and phone number, and can call the driver from the app.

This information is exchanged only for an active booking, so the two parties can identify one another and coordinate the pickup.

Our backend is powered by Google Cloud / Firebase infrastructure located in secure facilities. We do not sell your personal data under any circumstances. We only disclose information if strictly required by South African law or court order.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Under POPIA, we only process your personal information where we have a lawful basis to do so:

  • Your consent — given when you create an account and accept this policy, and which you may withdraw at any time.
  • Performance of our agreement with you — to connect you with a rider or driver and operate the trip you requested.
  • Legitimate interests — keeping the platform safe, preventing abuse, and improving the service, balanced against your privacy.
  • Legal obligation — where we must retain or disclose information to comply with South African law.

5. Account Deletion & Data Retention

You maintain full ownership of your data. LaynFleet provides clear paths to delete your account:

Immediate Web Deletion

Open the LaynRider account-deletion page, sign in with your LaynRider account, inspect the personal data linked to it, and confirm immediate permanent deletion.

In-App Deletion Request

Open More → Security Hub → Deregister inside LaynRider (or LaynDriver) and long-press the option to submit an in-app deletion request. The public web page remains available if you uninstall the app.

Cannot Sign In?

You may also request account erasure by emailing digilayn@gmail.com or messaging WhatsApp +27 81 553 2209 with your registered phone number.

De-identified trip records (for example, aggregate ride counts and fare statistics containing no personal identifiers) may be kept after erasure for safety auditing, dispute resolution, and service analytics, as permitted under POPIA.

6. Your Rights

Under POPIA (and related laws such as PAIA and ECTA), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you, and request a copy.
  • Correct or update information that is inaccurate or out of date (you can edit most of it directly in your in-app profile).
  • Request deletion of your account and personal data (see Section 5).
  • Object to or request that we restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 10. If you believe we have not handled your information lawfully, you may lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforegulator.org.za.

7. Children's Privacy

LaynFleet is intended for adults. You must be 18 years or older to create an account or use the platform. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If we become aware that we have collected data from a minor, we will delete it promptly.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the effective date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app or by email. Your continued use of LaynFleet after an update means you accept the revised policy.

9. Applicable Law

This Privacy Policy and our handling of your personal information are governed by the laws of the Republic of South Africa, including the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA), the Promotion of Access to Information Act 2 of 2000 (PAIA), and the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act 25 of 2002 (ECTA).

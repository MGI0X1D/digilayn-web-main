LaynFleet Privacy Policy

Your Privacy & Data Practices

This Privacy Policy explains how Digilayn collects, uses, and protects personal information across LaynRider and LaynDriver. We believe in minimal data collection, role-based separation, and total transparency.

Effective date: August 20, 2026 • Compliant with South Africa POPIA and Mobile App Store Standards.

🛡️ In Plain English (Summary)

  • ✓ No Data Selling: We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information or trip logs to third parties or advertising brokers.
  • ✓ Rider Location Privacy: Riders are never tracked in the background. GPS is only queried once when you tap "Use Current Location" or enter a pickup address.
  • ✓ Driver Live Location: Drivers share real-time location while "Online" or executing trips so riders and dispatch radar can see vehicle availability.
  • ✓ Right to Erasure: You can soft-delete your account directly in the app. Data is permanently purged within 30 days.

1. Information We Collect

🧑 LaynRider

Rider Information

  • Account Details: Mobile phone number (used for SMS/OTP verification) and optional name/profile handle.
  • Trip & Booking Data: Requested pickup location, destination, fare quotation, ride status, and timestamps.
  • On-Demand Location: Geographic coordinates obtained strictly when you tap "Use Current Location" to set your pickup point. We do not monitor your location when you are not actively setting a trip.
  • Feedback & Ratings: Ratings (1–5 stars) and optional written feedback submitted after completed trips.
🚗 LaynDriver

Driver Information

  • Driver Verification: Full name, phone number, vehicle details (make, model, color, registration/plate number), and driver documentation.
  • Live Location Tracking (Foreground & Background): While your driver status is toggled to "Online" or during an active trip, LaynDriver collects continuous GPS location. This enables the dispatch radar to broadcast your vehicle to nearby riders and calculate accurate ETAs.
  • Performance & Governance: Trip completion records, rider ratings, acceptance rates, and participation in fleet pricing votes.

2. How Your Information Is Used

We process data solely to operate and improve the LaynFleet transport ecosystem:

📍 Trip Dispatch & Routing

Connecting riders with available drivers, rendering map routes, and displaying live vehicle arrival updates.

🛡️ Safety & Mutual Trust

Displaying driver and vehicle details to confirmed riders, and verifying rider pickup identity.

🏷️ Fair Pricing & Governance

Calculating distance-based trip estimates and recording driver pricing votes.

🔧 Support & Debugging

Investigating booking issues, resolving ride disputes, and fixing application crashes.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Personal information is only shared between rider and driver for the duration of an active booking (e.g. driver sees pickup spot and rider name; rider sees driver name, photo, car plate, and live location).

Our backend is powered by Google Cloud / Firebase infrastructure located in secure facilities. We do not sell your personal data under any circumstances. We only disclose information if strictly required by South African law or court order.

🗑️

4. Account Deletion & Data Retention (30-Day Policy)

You maintain full ownership of your data. LaynFleet provides a clear and straightforward path to delete your account:

Step 1: In-App Soft Deletion

Navigate to Profile / Settings → Delete Account inside either LaynRider or LaynDriver. Once confirmed, your account is immediately deactivated and hidden from dispatch.

Step 2: 30-Day Grace & Permanent Erasure

Your profile, phone number, vehicle documents, and personal records are retained in a secured quarantine state for 30 days (to resolve any pending trip disputes or safety audits), after which all personal identifying data is permanently and irreversibly purged from our live database.

Alternative: Manual Deletion Request

You may also request account erasure by emailing digilayn@gmail.com or messaging WhatsApp +27 81 553 2209 with your registered phone number.

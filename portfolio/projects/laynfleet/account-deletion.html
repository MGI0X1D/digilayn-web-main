LaynDriver: request profile and account deletion

Email digilayn@gmail.com from your registered email address and request deletion of your LaynDriver account. If you cannot use that email address, contact WhatsApp +27 81 553 2209 for help verifying ownership. Do not send your password.

You can also open More → Security Hub → Deregister in LaynDriver to submit a deletion request. This suspends the account pending manager processing; it is not an immediate deletion.

Manager deletion removes your user and driver profiles, sign-in account, device records, reserved usernames and associated uploaded profile and licence files. Booking records are retained unchanged. They can contain account identifiers, pickup/drop-off addresses and coordinates, trip timestamps and notes. Profile deletion does not anonymise these records. They currently have no automatic expiry; ask support about retained data when requesting deletion.

The self-service sign-in below is for Rider accounts. Drivers should use the request options above.

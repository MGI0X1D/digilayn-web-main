Poortjie Operator

Poortjie Operator is an Android operational platform being built for small local transport operations. It focuses on the real workflow between operators, drivers, bookings, trip updates, and visibility.

The purpose of the build is to prove Digilayn’s ability to turn messy manual coordination into a structured system that supports daily business operations.

Operations Dashboard

Manage bookings, monitor workflow, and keep operational visibility.

Driver Assignment

Assign work to drivers and keep trip status updated.

Firebase Backend

Authentication, Firestore records, and cloud-backed workflows.

Notifications

Operational updates for key booking and trip events.

Poortjie Operator Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Digilayn collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Poortjie Operator mobile application and related services. By using Poortjie Operator, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Who We Are

Poortjie Operator is operated by Digilayn. The app is designed to help requesters, owners, and drivers manage local transport requests, driver assignments, trip information, and operational records.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information needed to provide and operate the app. This may include:

  • Account information, such as email address and user role.
  • Profile information entered in the app, such as name or operational details.
  • Booking and trip information, such as pickup address, destination address, booking status, assigned driver, trip records, and operational notes.
  • Driver location information where enabled for operational tracking.
  • Payment record information entered manually, such as cash or EFT status. The app does not process payments.
  • Device and notification information, such as Firebase Cloud Messaging tokens used to send push notifications.
  • Files or images uploaded through app features, if applicable.
  • Technical information needed for authentication, security, database operations, and app functionality.

3. Email-Based Accounts

Poortjie Operator uses email-based registration and login. Your email address is used to create your account, identify your access, support authentication, and communicate important account or operational information where necessary.

4. Location Information

Requesters may select pickup and destination addresses when creating transport requests. Owners may be able to view driver location for operational purposes. Driver location may be updated periodically, including approximately every 5 minutes while a driver is idling, to limit unnecessary data writes and battery usage. Location information is used to support pickup coordination, driver visibility, trip management, and transport operations.

5. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Authenticate users and protect access to the app.
  • Create, manage, update, and display booking requests.
  • Allow owners to assign drivers and manage transport operations.
  • Allow drivers to receive and manage assigned trips.
  • Send operational notifications such as booking updates, assignment updates, status changes, delays, or trip information.
  • Store operational records for users, owners, and drivers.
  • Improve app reliability, security, and performance.
  • Respond to support, deletion, and user requests.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, unauthorized access, or misuse of the app.

6. Firebase and Google Services

Poortjie Operator uses Firebase and Google services, including Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Storage, Cloud Functions, and Firebase Cloud Messaging. These services help us manage login, database records, uploaded content, backend operations, and push notifications. The app may also use Google Maps and Google Places services for map display, address search, place selection, and route-related functionality.

7. Analytics and Crash Reporting

Poortjie Operator does not currently use Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics. If analytics or crash reporting tools are added in future, this Privacy Policy and the Google Play Data Safety information will be updated to reflect that data collection.

8. Payments

Poortjie Operator does not process digital payments, card payments, wallet payments, or in-app purchases. The app may allow users to record payment-related information manually for operational tracking, such as whether a trip was paid by cash or EFT. Actual payment handling happens outside the app between the relevant users, drivers, owners, or transport providers.

9. Push Notifications

We may use Firebase Cloud Messaging to send push notifications related to booking requests, trip updates, driver assignment, operational changes, or account-related activity. You can control some notification permissions through your device settings.

10. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share or display information only as needed for app functionality, including:

  • Showing booking details to relevant requesters, owners, and drivers.
  • Showing driver assignment or driver location to relevant owners where operationally required.
  • Using Firebase and Google services to store, process, and deliver app functionality.
  • Responding to legal requests, safety concerns, fraud prevention, or security issues where required or permitted by law.

11. Data Storage and Security

User data is stored using Firebase and Google cloud infrastructure. We take reasonable steps to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. However, no online service, database, network, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Data Retention

We keep personal and operational information for as long as needed to provide the app, manage accounts, support transport operations, handle disputes, prevent fraud or abuse, comply with legal obligations, and maintain legitimate business records. When data is no longer needed, we may delete, anonymize, or archive it where appropriate.

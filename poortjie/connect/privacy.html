Poortjie Connect

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 17 May 2026

Poortjie Connect, a Digilayn product operated by Musa Mgijima ("we," "our," or "us"), respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

Poortjie Connect is a local community transport and errands platform designed for short-distance services within the Poortjie operational area. Services may include local passenger transport (including tuk-tuk transport), parcel collection, local errands, and delivery requests.

A tuk-tuk (also known as an auto-rickshaw) is a motorized three-wheeled vehicle commonly used for affordable short local transport.

By using Poortjie Connect, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Profile photo
  • Google account profile information

Booking Information

  • Pickup location
  • Destination location
  • Booking history
  • Request details

Technical Information

Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device token for notifications and basic app/device information required for functionality, diagnostics, and service reliability.

Profile Images

If you upload a profile photo, that image may be stored as part of your user profile.

2. How Location Information Works

Poortjie Connect does not continuously collect live device location in the background.

Location searches are performed through Google Places Autocomplete, which allows users to search for valid locations within the supported service area.

Poortjie Connect currently restricts searchable service locations to a defined local operating radius centered around Poortjie (currently approximately 50km).

3. How We Use Your Information

Create and manage your account
Authenticate sign-in
Process booking requests
Match users with local services
Send service notifications
Improve app functionality
Prevent abuse, fraud, or misuse
Support account deletion process

4. Third-Party Services

Google Services

We use Google services including Google Sign-In, Google Places Autocomplete, and Google Maps services.

These services may process relevant data according to Google's privacy policies.

Firebase Services

We use Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Firebase Cloud Messaging.

Firebase may process technical and authentication-related information required for platform functionality.

5. Notifications

If you enable notifications, Poortjie Connect may send notifications related to booking confirmations, updates, service status changes, and important account messages.

You can manage notification permissions through your device settings.

6. Account Deletion

You may request account deletion from within the app. When completed:

  • Your authentication account is deleted
  • Your user profile data is removed
  • Associated application data is removed where applicable

Minimal deletion-related audit records may be retained for security and operational auditing.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share limited information only when necessary to provide requested services, process bookings, operate platform infrastructure, comply with legal obligations, or protect users and the service.

8. Children's Privacy

Poortjie Connect is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect personal information using modern platform infrastructure and access controls. However, no digital service can guarantee absolute security.

10. Service Area Limitation

Poortjie Connect is intended for local use within supported operational regions only. Service availability may be geographically restricted.
